# 全人类故事模式菜单v2
## 本模组面向shady-loader/shady-lua开发，且仅在v2.10以上的版本方能正常运行。

## 相比v1，新的功能：
- 将来自绯想天的15条角色故事线稍作修复后复刻到非想天则中，并**扩展原版故事模式菜单**，让这些角色变得可以选择。

  现在您无需打开绯想天，便能直接在非想天则中游玩绯想天的故事模式！

- 相应地，"Result"菜单中，所有角色故事线中的符卡也将向您开放，前提是您已经解锁了相关的故事线。

- 除绯想天和非想天则的原有剧情外，本模组还加入了@Ikutron为灵乌路空与诹访子创作的同人故事线，以保证主玩该两位角色的玩家的体验。

  灵乌路空将对战琪露诺、早苗与诹访子；诹访子将先后与非想天则其他八名角色战斗，不过她拥有无敌的遁地，祝她好运！

## 关于存档：
如果您的score123.dat不是由游戏初始化生成的，而是替换自全开档，那么Result菜单中可能出现不能访问绯想天角色的问题。这是因为普遍使用的全开档疑似存在损坏。

解决方法是删除游戏根目录下你现有的score123.dat（`th123\score123.dat`），这会导致你的故事通关记录、已解锁故事、已解锁角色、已解锁卡片、MusicRoom解锁和地图解锁全部丢失。
当然，更推荐的做法是使用本mod随附的score123-fullsc.dat或score123-init.dat替换原有的score123.dat。
> - 对于fullsc后缀的dat版本，其拥有完全解锁的卡片、角色、故事线等，还包括**全部的故事符卡记录**（这样您就能在Result菜单随意练习任何一张故事符卡）
> - 对于init后缀的dat版本，其故事部分完全初始，您将可以游玩灵梦、魔理沙、咲夜和早苗的故事模式，并随着通关解锁更多的故事线，以还原绯想天中的体验。



## 注意事项
- 来自绯想天中的角色中，灵梦、爱丽丝、魔理沙与帕秋莉本身已在非想天则登场，因此，她们的绯想天故事模式符卡已经被非想天则故事中的符卡顶替且暂时无法还原。
- 由于涉及到本地化和版本差异，仓库提供以下三个分支，请根据需求选择相应的版本下载。
  1. zh

  3. en-th123e

  若你因为某些原因，未使用th123intl插件实现本地化（如使用th123e等），请使用带有"th123e"后缀的分支。
  若你正常使用th123intl插件，则不需要考虑。

# FullCharacterStoryMode v2

## This mod is designed for shady-loader / shady-lua and requires version 2.10 or later to function properly.

## New features compared to v1:

* Restores the 15 character story routes from SWR with minor fixes and **extends the original Story Mode menu**, allowing these characters to become selectable.

  You no longer need to launch SWR just to play SWR story mode — now you can experience it directly inside Soku!

* Accordingly, the spell cards from all character story routes are now also available in the Result Menu, provided that you have cleared the corresponding story route.

* In addition to the original stories from SWR and Soku, this mod also includes fan-made story routes created by @Ikutron for Utsuho and Suwako to improve the experience for players who mainly use these two characters.

  Utsuho will fight Cirno, Sanae, and Suwako.

  Suwako will fight the other eight Soku characters in sequence — fortunately, she can hide underground to dodge spell cards. Good luck to her.

---

## About Save Files

If your `score123.dat` was not generated by the game itself and instead comes from a downloaded complete save, you may encounter issues where SWR characters become inaccessible from the Result Menu.

This is because commonly circulated complete saves appear to contain corrupted data.

The solution is to delete your existing `score123.dat` located in the game directory (`th123\score123.dat`).

Doing so will cause you to lose:

* Story clear records
* Unlocked story routes
* Unlocked characters
* Unlocked cards
* Music Room unlock progress
* Stage unlock progress

Of course, the recommended approach is replacing your existing `score123.dat` with either `score123-fullsc.dat` or `score123-init.dat`, both included with this mod.

> * The `fullsc` save version contains fully unlocked cards, characters, story routes, etc., including **all story-mode spell card records**, allowing you to freely practice any story spell card from the Result Menu.
>
> * The `init` save version starts with story progression completely reset. You will initially have access to the story modes of Reimu, Marisa, Sakuya, and Sanae, unlocking more routes as you progress, recreating the original SWR experience.

## Notes

* Among the characters originating from SWR, Reimu, Alice, Marisa, and Patchouli already appear in Soku.

  Therefore, the spell cards from their SWR story routes unfortunately have been replaced by their spell cards from Soku, and restoring their original SWR versions would require a disproportionate amount of work.

* Due to localization and version differences, the repository provides the following three branches. Please download the version appropriate for your setup:

  1. zh
  2. en
  3. en-th123e

  If, for any reason, you are not using the `th123intl` plugin for localization (for example, if you use `th123e`), please use branches with the `"th123e"` suffix.

  If you are using `th123intl` normally, you should just choose non-suffixed branch.
